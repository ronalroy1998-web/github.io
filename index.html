FSR sensors / ESP32 / Sensor integration / Serial data visualisation

What the prototype demonstrates +

The team integrated force-sensitive resistors with analogue inputs and positioned sensors on an insole. The supplied demonstration shows the physical setup and displayed sensor signals.

This is a proof-of-concept acquisition setup. Quantitative accuracy and clinical performance have not been established in the presented materials. Calibration, noise reduction, packaging and wireless transmission are areas for further development.
